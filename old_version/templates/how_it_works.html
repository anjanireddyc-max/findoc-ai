HOW IT WORKS

From document to AI explanation.

FinDocAI combines image classification, document region analysis and Grad-CAM visualization into one workflow.

01

Upload or capture

Provide a receipt or invoice through an image upload or directly through your device camera.

02

Divide into regions

Large documents are divided into smaller image regions for localized analysis.

03

EfficientNetV2B0 analysis

Each region is evaluated by the trained EfficientNetV2B0 model to estimate suspicious visual patterns.

04

Grad-CAM explanation

Grad-CAM generates an attention map showing which visual areas contributed strongly to the prediction.

05

Final classification

Regional scores are combined into a document-level result of Genuine or Tampered.
