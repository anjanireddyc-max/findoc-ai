What these numbers mean.

These results are measured on complete test documents that the model never saw during training, exactly the way the Detect page analyses an uploaded document. Out of {{ metrics.tampered }} tampered documents, {{ metrics.tp }} were detected and {{ metrics.fn }} were missed; out of {{ metrics.documents - metrics.tampered }} genuine documents, {{ metrics.fp }} were wrongly flagged. A document is reported as tampered when its most unusual region reaches {{ metrics.threshold }} (method: {{ metrics.method }}).
